Test rules
  1. One attempt only. There are no retakes. If your browser closes you may resume, but the timer keeps running.
  2. Stay in fullscreen on this tab for the whole test. Do not open other tabs, windows or applications.
  3. No copying or pasting, no right-click, no developer tools, no printing or saving the page.
  4. Work alone. No phones, notes, second screens, other people or AI tools.
  5. The server keeps the time. Answers autosave. When time ends the test is submitted automatically.
  6. Stages are sequential. You must reach the qualifying score of each stage to unlock the next.
  7. Use a desktop browser with a stable connection.
Warnings & disqualification

Each of the following is logged as a warning:

  • Leaving fullscreen
  • Switching tab or window / losing focus
  • Copy, cut or paste attempts
  • Opening developer tools

You will be removed from the test when:

  • You reach the maximum number of warnings — your test is submitted immediately and flagged for review.
  • The administrator finds malpractice in the proctoring log: impersonation, outside help, a second device, or tampering with the test page. This results in disqualification from the whole drive.
  • Your score is below the qualifying score — you are not eligible for later stages.

Every warning is time-stamped by the server and visible to the administrator. Resizing the window is recorded but is not counted as a warning.
